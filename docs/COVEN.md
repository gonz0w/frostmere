# The Grove That Refused Spring

The Briar Coven initiation is playable after claiming the woodland-memory reward at the Stag Shrine. Speak with healer Maelin to accept the trial and receive six herbs, six water and three Amber Resin. Restore the west, east and south settlement seedbeds; each consumes two herbs, two water and one resin and reveals green shoots. These beds stand outside the sacred grove, preserving either earlier wild or tended pact.

Return to Maelin and explicitly choose an oath. Shelter renewal grants four Heartwood; share medicine grants eight herbs. Both grant Seedkeeper rank, 100 gold, a Fine favorited Seedkeeper Charm and its forge pattern. The charm has two armor, +2 Endurance and +2 Willpower. Forge cost: three Heartwood, two Amber Resin and one Silver Ingot. Standard tempering, enchantment and socket rules apply.

The journal, faction dossier and woodland HUD show progress. Optional roots.coven_started, coven_beds and coven_oath fields preserve the trial in older-compatible saves. Validation requires the woodland reward, unique valid seedbeds and all three beds before the oath. Physical site checks and single-claim rewards prevent remote tending and duplicate supplies.

This is a compact first initiation. The promise currently affects dialogue and material rewards; it does not simulate growing crops or distributing medicine. Broader faction consequences remain roadmap work.

## A Debt of Sap and Stone

After becoming a Seedkeeper, ask Maelin about the lodge. Inspect its foundation at local (18, 0, 30), inspect the existing Spring root, and record Oren's agreement. Return to Maelin with all three findings. Either solution keeps the settlement in place and preserves the previous woodland pact and membership oath.

Choose a raised timber crossing to protect root space or a lined catchment cistern to reduce runoff. Both cost four Heartwood and two Amber Resin. Missing supplies leave evidence and inventory intact. The chosen repair appears beside the lodge; these are decorative first-pass structures, not new collision surfaces or simulated water flow.

Completion grants Pactmender rank, 140 gold and a Superior favorited Pactmender Ring plus its forge pattern. The ring has two base armor, +3 Endurance and +2 Presence. Forge cost: three Heartwood, two Amber Resin, one Silver Ingot. Tempering uses Heartwood and Amber Resin; standard enchanting and sockets apply.

Optional debt_started, debt_evidence and debt_choice fields preserve progress. Validation rejects missing membership, duplicate or unknown evidence and premature agreements. Dialogue proximity and one-time costs/rewards are enforced. The journal, Coven dossier and woodland HUD show progress. The Last Old Promise now completes the first playable Coven arc.

## The Last Old Promise

After resolving the lodge debt, ask Maelin to begin the final review. Record her case for renewed protection, Oren's shared charter and the woodland memory's warning. Visit all three physical sites, then decide at the Heart of the Stag. Both choices forbid consuming the grove to power a crossing and preserve the original woodland pact, membership oath, lodge repair and Lantern progress.

Renew the old protections for six Heartwood, or adopt a shared charter for three Heartwood and three Amber Resin. Both grant Rootwarden rank, 200 gold and a Superior favorited Old Promise Talisman plus its forge pattern. The amulet has three armor, +3 Endurance, +3 Willpower and +2 Presence. Forge with four Heartwood, three Amber Resin and two Silver Ingots. Tempering uses Heartwood/resin; normal enchantment and socket rules apply.

Optional promise_started, promise_heard and promise_choice fields save the review and permanent decision. Validation requires debt completion and unique testimony from all three sources. Decisions require dialogue proximity at the Heart and cannot grant rewards again. The journal, faction dossier and woodland HUD track the finale.

All three Coven chapters now have compact playable passes. The final policy currently changes recorded dialogue, rank and supply rewards; yearly governance is not simulated. The earlier outline's archive-burning lockout is not part of this implementation. Richer encounters and broader faction consequences remain expansion work.

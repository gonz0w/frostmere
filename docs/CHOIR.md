# A Song Through the Stone

Ember Caldera is a playable main-story chapter after **The Divided Council**. Take the southeastern ash pilgrimage from Frostmere. A council resolution is required; membership in either clan or the Ashen Choir is not. Sereth acknowledges a clan allegiance without refusing the investigation.

## Sanctuary investigation

Speak with **Cantor Sereth** in the southern pilgrim lodge. Four Ashbound Sentinels patrol the sanctuary. Read the three numbered inscriptions and ring the resonators in this order: **Ember → Ash → Stone**. They use three different pitches and light up as the sequence advances. Wrong notes reset the sequence without consuming materials. The full puzzle is solvable with audio muted, and a partial correct sequence survives saving.

Repair the receiver with **three Resonant Ore and two crystals** after completing the sequence. Each of the two supply chests contains four ore, three Ash Glass and two crystals, so either chest provides enough repair materials. Repair locks the song, lowers the vault's physical barrier and makes the **Dissonant Cantor** active and vulnerable.

Defeat the guardian, loot its blade, copy the protected distress signal and return to Sereth. The transmission reveals that the awakening song is a stranded crew's emergency beacon, with an explicit warning to keep the furnace sealed. The report records `choir` in story progress. The woodland, shore, lens and Choir discoveries now all have playable investigation chapters. Combining them in **The Roads Converge** is now playable at Mirewatch; the space expedition and subsequent Aeralis chapter are now playable in normal progression. No later realm gate is bypassed by this report.

## Travel and persistence

The volcanic setting includes an enterable lodge, workshop shelter, basalt sanctuary, organ-like columns, three resonators and a guarded signal vault. Lava channels are decorative scenery behind solid parapets and end barriers. They do not introduce a damage or mining system.

The local map marks Sereth, supplies, workstations, resonators, receiver and archive. Exploration/battle music uses the existing Nordic tracks. The resonator sound reuses the existing enchant effect at three pitches through the Effects bus, so it respects effects/master volume controls.

Acceptance, partial song, receiver repair, copied signal, rewards, caches and enemy/loot progress are saved in an optional `choir` record. Validation rejects out-of-order songs, duplicate entries, missing prerequisites and contradictory completion states before replacing the character. Older saves start with untouched Choir progress. Leaving resets surviving enemies and restores the previous environment; death safely returns home with restored vitals.

**Preview Ember Caldera.command** launches a temporary post-council character with a Superior steel sword of Fleet, bow, arrows and healing draughts. Character and atlas saving are both disabled throughout the preview. E interacts, F heals, V changes camera, and M opens the local map.

The Ashen Choir initiation and both promotions are playable; see [Listener](CHOIR_LISTENER.md), [Cantor](ASH_MEMORY.md) and [Ember Voice](FIRE_INVITE.md). See [the current content reference](CONTENT_REFERENCE.md) for catalog totals and quest availability. There has been no timed human pacing study or new M1 performance benchmark for this chapter.


## Voices in the Furnace

Sereth now offers a separate ash-pilgrimage discussion after the main signal reward. Three new shrines and the omitted names lead to optional Listener membership. Main resonator progress, campaign frequency and Iron Pact standing are preserved. See CHOIR_LISTENER.md.


## Ash Remembers Everything

Listeners can ask Sereth about sealed awakening records. The northern archive cabinet opens through its separate pressure lock and reveals the elder council’s order. The permanent publication/concealment decision grants Cantor rank while preserving the main signal and Iron Pact standing. See ASH_MEMORY.md.


## The Fire We Invite

Cantors can now decide the local captive ember’s fate after securing both governors. Ember Voice outcomes have distinct Masterwork rewards and permanent cross-faction consequences. See FIRE_INVITE.md.


## A Pilgrim’s Empty Shoes

The missing-pilgrim notice beside the entrance opens an independent side quest before any Choir oath. Leth’s staged escape leads to shelter or supplies for departure; either places the pilgrim safely at camp. See EMPTY_SHOES.md.

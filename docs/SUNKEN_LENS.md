# The Sky Below the Sand

The Sunken Lens is a playable observatory chapter after **The Missing Atlas**. Follow the eastern expedition road from Frostmere. The Black Shore flight recorder provides the narrative lead, but this branch remains parallel in the atlas: completing the coastal chapter or choosing a particular core destination is not required.

## Expedition

Speak with **Astronomer Veyra** at the southern camp. Enter the circular observatory and use the inscriptions to rotate the three mirrors clockwise through North, East, South and West. Each rotation changes the physical mirror and its visible beam. Dawn must face East, Dusk West, and Crown North. The local map also shows correctly aligned beams.

Both excavation caches sit outside the observatory walls. Each contains four Optic Glass, two crystals and four Solar Alloy. Once all mirrors are aligned, restore the central lens for exactly three Optic Glass and two crystals. Missing materials spend nothing. The activated lens locks the mirrors, illuminates its heart and lowers the rear vault's physical barrier.

Four Gilded Sentinels guard the observatory. The **Meridian Keeper** remains protected and inactive until the lens is restored. Defeat it, loot its blade, recover the protected star chart, then report to Veyra. Defeated enemies, loot, rotations, activation, the chart and the reward are saved. Departure resets surviving enemies and restores the previous environment; combat death safely returns home.

Veyra acknowledges either coastal choice: navigation confirms the Crown's northern bearing, while the harbor route keeps Saltbreak's beacon alive. Both retain the observatory lead. The recovered chart points toward the First Threshold; the woodland and Choir branches are playable, convergence is now playable at Mirewatch, and the First Threshold expedition now opens Aeralis through normal progression.

The report also grants 160 gold, seven Solar Alloy, five Optic Glass, five steel ingots, and **Sunlance**. The spell becomes selected immediately. All three equipment patterns use the existing forge; gear supports the existing tempering, enchantment, gem, favorite and salvage systems. Solar Alloy and steel support tempering; high ranks also require Optic Glass. Supplies and enemy loot are finite.

## Sunlance

Sunlance costs **30 magicka** with a **1.5-second cooldown**. Its aimed golden projectile deals **70% of spell power**, passes through one enemy, then deals **70% of the original projectile damage before the target's damage reduction** to a second target. It cannot hit the same target twice or reach a third target. Solid walls stop it. Other spells and arrows keep their previous collision rules.

Use Q to cast the selected spell, B to cycle learned spells, or left-click with a normal staff/focus. Sunlance remains learned outside the observatory and survives save/load. It does not require keeping the Horizon Staff equipped.

## Preview and limits

Double-click **Preview Sunken Lens.command** for an isolated post-atlas character. Character and atlas persistence are both disabled. Press E to interact and M for the local map. Existing Nordic exploration/battle music responds to the chapter's enemies.

The prototype now contains **133 gear designs, 149 recipes, ten enchantment formulas and five defined spell abilities** (Chain Lightning remains an equipment-specific ability). Seventeen of the 51 atlas quests are normally playable; 34 remain outlines. The space expedition and its four prerequisite branches are now playable. No timed human pacing or new M1 performance measurement has been made for this chapter.

## Optional debt investigation

Ask Veyra about her missing instruments to begin The Astronomer's Debt. The western and eastern cache interactions now also offer instrument recovery after their respective sentinels (lens_0 and lens_1) are defeated. Earlier defeats count, and excavation supplies remain independently collectible. Return both instruments to choose debt settlement or public research, with different upgrade-material/gold rewards and recorded dialogue consequences. See ASTRONOMER_DEBT.md for the exact outcomes.

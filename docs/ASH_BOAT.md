# The Boat That Would Not Burn

> Verification counts and capture observations below are historical development evidence, not fresh checks. `build/` paths refer to local output omitted from the source checkout; see [evidence guidance](README.md#screenshots-and-development-evidence).

`side_ashport` is playable after the archive unlocks coastal travel. Speak to dockworker Brann near Saltbreak’s southern road. An Ashport launch has washed onto the existing black shore; this quest does not add a separate Ashport town.

Inspect the charred launch and heat-sealed manifest, then record Brann’s and archivist Cerys’s claims. The plates were purchased for public shelters and borrowed for a furnace trial. Brann wants them returned; Cerys needs the originals to study their furnace-control inscriptions. Return to the boat for the permanent award.

- Workers: 140 gold, Superior Vigor Breakwater Mantle named “Ashport’s Shelter,” and 6 steel ingots.
- Choir: 140 gold, Superior Insight Signal Focus named “Fireproof Memory,” 6 Resonant Ore and 4 Ash Glass.

Both equipment rewards are favorites. The chest disappears after the award and the journal records the outcome. Cargo delivery and shelter/research consequences are narrative outcomes; no transport simulation or new faction rank is granted. The main shore quest, Compact arc and Choir membership remain independent.

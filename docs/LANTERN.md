# The Uncatalogued Door

The Veiled Lantern's first membership quest is playable after resolving The Missing Atlas. Speak to Mereth in Pinehall, accept the trial, revisit the archivist's testimony, then use the Uncatalogued Door at local (0, 0, -53) in the second archive chamber. The door is a framed interactive record seal; it does not open another traversable dungeon wing.

The trial asks what survives conflicting accounts. Preserving the complete testimony is the answer; wrong answers provide a clue and allow retry without penalty. Return the recovered protected testimony to Mereth and explicitly take the oath to become a Witness. Returning without taking the oath remains possible.

Either prior atlas custodian permits membership. The custody choice, reputation and original exclusive relic pattern remain intact. Lantern initiation grants 100 gold, a Fine favorited Witness Pendant and its forge pattern. The pendant has two base armor, +2 Intellect and +2 Willpower. Forge cost: two Silver Ingots, three Memory Shards, one Crystal. Tempering uses silver and memory shards; normal enchanting and sockets apply.

The optional archive.lantern_trial stage saves investigation and membership. Older profiles default to no trial. Stage values are validated and any started trial requires completed atlas custody. Interactions require the correct physical site and dialogue; the reward is single-claim. The quest journal and Lantern faction dossier show live progress.

Names Beneath the Water is now playable; The Price of Knowing is now playable. Joining currently grants the Witness rank and gear; broader faction politics and later rival capstone exclusions are not implemented here.

## Names Beneath the Water

As a Witness, accept Mereth's next investigation. Reach Mirewatch through the normal main-story expedition gates. Recover the passenger list at (-23, 0, 30), relief register at (7, 0, 7), and sealed evacuation testimony at (15, 0, -55), relative to Mirewatch's origin. The northern testimony requires the return beacon to be calibrated; collection is free, protected quest evidence and cannot repeat. Existing expedition mechanics and enemies remain unchanged.

Return all three records to Mereth. Publishing restores the missing names publicly but identifies surviving witnesses; sealing preserves the full testimony while withholding public answers. The recorded choice changes Mereth's dialogue, journal and exclusive gear reward; broader NPC/world reactions are not yet simulated.

Either resolution grants Recordkeeper rank, 140 gold and a Superior favorited ring plus its forge pattern. Public: Memorial Ring, two armor, +3 Presence/+2 Endurance. Sealed: Sealed Memory Ring, two armor, +3 Intellect/+2 Willpower. Only the chosen pattern unlocks. Both cost three Silver Ingots, three Memory Shards and two Crystal to forge. Tempering uses silver/memory shards; normal enchanting and sockets apply.

Optional archive names_accepted, names_records and names_choice fields preserve investigation and resolution. Save validation rejects unknown/duplicate records, collection without membership/acceptance, northern proof before beacon calibration, and resolution without all evidence. Earlier archive saves default to an unstarted investigation.

## The Price of Knowing

After resolving the Mirewatch testimony, ask Mereth to begin the final review. Record Mereth's argument for controlled study and Aelin's argument for access or shared oversight. Return to the Uncatalogued Door with both perspectives and choose:

- Seal the archive: controlled study; six Memory Shards.
- Share the archive: public copying; six Living Resin.
- Shared oversight: neither faction acts alone; three of each material.

Every choice grants Keeper rank, 200 gold, a Superior favorited Lantern Keystone and its forge pattern. The amulet has three base armor, +3 Intellect/+3 Willpower/+2 Presence. Forge cost: three Silver Ingots, four Memory Shards, two Living Resin. Temper with silver and memory shards; normal enchantment/socket rules apply.

The prior testimony/custodian choices are preserved. Mereth, Aelin and the archive-seal dialogue reflect the final policy; the faction dossier and quest journal show Keeper progress. This first pass does not simulate visitors, patrols or public copying, and does not lock out the Coven capstone. Optional price_started, price_heard and price_choice fields validate prerequisites, unique consultation evidence and completed decisions. Physical dialogue proximity and one-time claims are enforced.

All three Lantern chapters now have compact playable implementations. Broader consequences, new dungeon spaces, creature encounters and richer quest presentation remain expansion work.

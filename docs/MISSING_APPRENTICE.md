# The Apprentice Who Vanished

A compact Pinehall side quest, available after Stonewake opens the western trail. Read the college notice near the entrance, follow Iven's marginal research at the archivist's testimony, then find him in the rear archive alcove. Defeat archive_2 before helping him return; earlier defeats count. The existing atlas, faction custody and Lantern trials remain independent.

The rescue interaction relocates Iven to camp beside the notice and returns control to the player. His nameplate and interaction prompt change to returned apprentice. This is a dialogue-resolved safe return, not a walking escort simulation. Speak with him in camp to decide the research's future:

- Return it for supervised college study, with Iven's authorship recorded: Iven's Field Rod, a Superior crystal staff enchanted with Insight, plus the Insight formula.
- Keep it for independent healing research: Iven's Field Robe, a Superior cloth robe enchanted with Vigor, plus the Vigor formula.

Either grants 100 gold and protects the reward as a favorite. These are personalized instances of existing equipment designs; normal tempering, enchantment and socket rules apply. Known formulas are not duplicated. The choice is recorded in dialogue and journal; no college simulation or treatment system is added.

Optional apprentice_stage (0–3) and apprentice_choice fields live in archive progress. Stage order, guard proof, physical proximity and single-use rewards are checked. Missing fields default to unstarted for older profiles; malformed or inconsistent progress is rejected before loading changes. Iven's location derives from saved progress, so revisits and reloads preserve the return.

# The Roads Converge

Mirewatch is a playable expedition-preparation chapter in Glassfen. Entry requires all four story completions: **Pacts Beneath the Roots**, **The Wreck on the Black Shore**, **The Sky Below the Sand**, and **A Song Through the Stone**. Take the southeastern expedition road from Frostmere. No faction membership is required.

## Briefing, forging and calibration

Speak with **Ilyra** in the stilt lodge. Record all four findings at the evidence desks on the southern platform. Each briefing light turns green when recorded. The woodland account acknowledges either the wild or tended pact, while the wreck briefing acknowledges either harbor power or restored navigation. All four combinations retain the expedition route.

Recording all evidence unlocks the **Transit Regulator** blueprint. Collect the crew's reserved supplies: eight steel ingots, six crystals, four rune dust and four Ash Glass. At the expedition forge, search for **Transit Regulator**. Forging one consumes **four steel, three crystals and two rune dust**. The crew provides enough for two; only one is required, and spare materials can be used elsewhere.

Follow the boardwalk north and install one regulator at the calibration rig. This consumes the crafted item and awakens two survey sentinels and **the Misbound Navigator**. They remain dormant and immune before installation. Defeat all three, recover their upgrade supplies and stabilize the return beacon. Report to Ilyra to complete `convergence`.

The expedition uses a closed crystal reserve rather than living land or the sealed furnace. The briefing combines the chart's destination, the recorder's transit data, the Choir's beacon frequency and the woodland warning. This milestone prepares the crossing; **Beyond the First Threshold is now playable from the Sunken Lens vault**. Neither Aeralis nor the Crucible unlocks early.

Reporting also grants 180 gold, eight steel ingots, six crystals and four Ash Glass. Each equipment recipe costs four steel and two crystals. Both designs support tempering, enchanting, sockets, favorites and salvage. Tempering uses steel and crystal; Exquisite and higher also require Ash Glass. Sentinel loot supplies additional steel, crystal and glass once per enemy. Crew supplies and enemy loot are finite.

## Traversal, saves and preview

Two broad timber platforms stand on stilts above the marsh, joined by a boardwalk. The southern platform holds Ilyra's lodge, evidence desks, forge, rune table and supply chest. The northern platform holds the calibration rig, sentinels and return beacon. Falling into the marsh returns the player to the arrival dock with velocity cleared. Combat death returns home and restores vitals. Departing resets surviving enemies and restores the previous environment.

The optional `convergence` save record stores acceptance, evidence, supply collection, installation, calibration, rewards, defeated enemies and claimed loot. A crafted regulator uses ordinary material inventory and survives saving. Validation rejects missing prerequisite completions, duplicate/unknown evidence, defeat before installation, calibration before all defenders are defeated, invalid loot and incomplete reward dependencies. Older saves receive untouched expedition progress.

**Preview Mirewatch.command** starts an isolated character with all preceding branches complete, a wild woodland pact and harbor-power outcome. Both character and atlas persistence are disabled. E interacts, I opens crafting, M shows the local map, V changes camera and F uses a healing draught. Exploration and combat use the existing music and interaction effects.

See [the current content reference](CONTENT_REFERENCE.md) for catalog totals and quest availability. No timed human pacing study or new M1 performance benchmark has been performed for this chapter.

## Supply-shortage fallback

After learning the Transit Regulator design and collecting the original supplies, the supply chest offers a dedicated spare-parts rebuild if you lack both the component and enough ordinary ingredients. This produces one Transit Regulator without raw materials, gold or XP. Owning the component or finishing its installation removes the option. Use the existing quest action to install/bind it; other gates remain intact. No save-schema change is needed.

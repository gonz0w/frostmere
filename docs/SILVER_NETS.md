# Nets Full of Silver

A compact Saltbreak investigation available after The Missing Atlas opens the coastal road. Ask Sella about the missing crew, inspect the coin-marked fishing net on the western dock, then recover the smuggler's ledger from the existing sea-cave cache after defeating shore_1. Earlier defeats count. The ledger reveals that the crew hid from Brinecutter threats; Sella's runners locate them, and the player decides how to report the evidence.

- Publish the full ledger: the crew returns to testify publicly; 120 gold.
- Protect the crew's identities: the smuggling route is published with their names removed; 60 gold and six Silver Ingots.

Both outcomes grant a Fine, favorited Netmender's Band and its forge pattern. The ring has two base armor, +2 Endurance and +2 Presence, and weighs 0.1 kg. At Fine its armor is four. Its recipe costs two Silver Ingots, two Leather and one Crystal. Tempering uses silver and leather, with crystals required at higher ranks; normal enchantment and socket rules apply.

The investigation has separate saved silver_stage (0–3) and silver_choice fields inside shore progress. Old profiles default to unstarted. Stage order, guard proof, physical proximity and one-time reward rules are checked. Evidence cannot be sold or consumed as crafting materials. Main coastal caches, repairs, core choices and chapter rewards remain separate.

The net has visible silver coins and a woven grid; the ledger is a separate prop on the cave cache. Secured evidence disappears. Sella and the journal retain the chosen story outcome. Crew recovery is described in dialogue; there is no new refuge interior, escort sequence or simulated harbor economy.



## Tidemarch Compact initiation

Arbiter Vessa and a departure register stand beside the southern road. The guarded coastal nameplate and Neris’s account support a separate Compact initiation. Neither core outcome nor the silver-net quest is altered. See TIDE_COMPACT.md.


## The Harbor That Lied

The Compact’s second chapter now opens at Vessa after the Decksworn oath. Compare the registry, recover orders east of the cave exit, clear shore_2 and arrange Tamsin’s shelter before presentation. Relocation is interaction-resolved, not a walking escort. See HARBOR_LIED.md.


## The Price of Safe Passage

All three Compact ranks are now playable. Envoys can draft the passage charter with Vessa, consult existing NPCs through separate discussion options and sign at the southern charter table. Old coastal dialogues remain accessible. See SAFE_PASSAGE.md.


## The Boat That Would Not Burn

Brann and Cerys now offer an independent Ashport cargo dispute after archive access. The beached launch, manifest, saved evidence, permanent award and disappearing cargo are implemented. Main shore and Compact progression are unchanged. See ASH_BOAT.md.


## The Keeper’s Last Light

Relief pilot Mara, the keeper’s log and independent ruined signal controls now support The Keeper’s Last Light. Repairs cost 2 iron and 1 crystal; signaling relocates a visual ship to safe anchor. Main beacon and faction progress stay independent. See LAST_LIGHT.md.


## Final seven quests

Three Inns, One Secret connects Pell in Frostmere, Bessa in Saltbreak and Corin in Wyrmkeep. Recover the route book after clearing the Brinecutter, bring Venn to the guesthouse and choose public or protected evidence. Venn and his label relocate together. The Concord rivalry now blocks actual Compact finale actions in both orders. See ROAD_QUESTS.md.

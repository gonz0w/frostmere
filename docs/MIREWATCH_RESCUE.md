# Lights Beneath the Boards

A compact optional side quest on Mirewatch's existing western lodge deck. The normal road requires Roots, Tides, Lens and Choir; no expedition acceptance, regulator, sentinel fight or faction membership is required for this side quest.

Speak to Ilyra and ask about the lights. Investigate the signal at the northwest deck corner, release the jammed service-vault winch halfway down the western deck, then open Tovin's cache at the southwest corner. Each action uses E and a dialogue button at its physical location. The vault rescue is resolved through the winch interaction, not a new explorable underground dungeon.

Tovin appears beside the winch after rescue. His moving signal reflection blinks in groups of three and stops when he is safe. The cache lid opens after collection. Original dry humor accompanies the investigation and rescue. The journal displays the current objective; the main expedition remains independent.

Reward: 90 gold, six crystals, four rune dust and two healing draughts. Rescue consumes no materials. Rewards are single-use and cannot be claimed before investigation/rescue or remotely from a stale dialogue. Optional rescue_stage in the existing convergence save record stores stages 0–4; missing fields default to unstarted. Invalid stages or progress without Mirewatch access prerequisites are rejected before loading changes.

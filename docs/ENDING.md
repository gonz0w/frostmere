# The Last Furnace: shutdown and homecoming

The Crucible now has an explicit final objective and a replayable ending chronicle. Defeat both sentries and the Furnace Maw, recover the Warden's relic, and use the emergency shutdown console at `(6, 0, -36)` on the final terrace. The engine core and its warning lamps go dark. Ambient lava and distant furnace scenery remain visible.

Returning through the arrival gate after shutdown completes The Last Furnace. A homecoming panel pauses play and reflects the player's woodland pact, harbor decision and clan membership when those choices exist. Close it to continue adventuring. A chronicle beside the home-world realm gates at `(41, 0, -35)` reopens the ending and any unclaimed reward. Completed repeat return trips do not reopen a claimed reward screen.

## Heirlooms

Choose one Exquisite, favorited heirloom of Vigor. Every choice grants all three Dawnforged patterns, 350 gold, 12 steel, 8 crystals, 8 Cinder Alloy and 6 Soul Embers.

| Design | Base damage | Attribute bonus |

| Dawnforged Sword | 29 | +4 Strength |
| Dawnforged Bow | 30 | +4 Agility |
| Dawnforged Staff | 32 | +4 Intellect |

The selected piece is granted once. Each pattern costs 4 steel, 3 Cinder Alloy, 2 crystals and 2 Soul Embers. Steel and Cinder Alloy support further tempering; higher ranks require Soul Embers. Gear uses the established weapon families and an icy green tint. You can forge the other designs, enchant and socket them, and use them across the existing regions.

## Persistence and compatibility

Shutdown and heirloom choice save with the character. Invalid reward choices, inconsistent completion and malformed field types are rejected before state is applied. Shutdown survives leaving and returning. Earlier saves that already completed The Last Furnace migrate with shutdown satisfied and the new heirloom unclaimed; earlier unfinished saves receive the shutdown objective. There is no forced restart.

`Preview Last Furnace.command` starts the existing isolated Crucible setup with infernal armor, a machine gun, ammunition and Soulfire. Character and atlas saving stay disabled, including after the ending. `Play Frostmere.command` continues the regular profile.

The game now has 133 equipment designs and 149 crafting recipes. Seventeen of 51 atlas quests are playable; the remaining 34 outlines and further environment, encounter and narrative expansion are still future work. This addition supplies an ending to the connected prototype campaign, not a claim that the full game is finished. No new M1 benchmark or human pacing study was performed.

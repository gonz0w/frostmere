# The final seven quests

> Verification counts and capture observations below are historical development evidence, not fresh checks. `build/` paths refer to local output omitted from the source checkout; see [evidence guidance](README.md#screenshots-and-development-evidence).

All seven remaining atlas quest outlines are playable. The atlas now contains 51 playable quests and zero planned quests. This completes the quest-implementation list; it does not declare all world expansion, polish or release work finished.

These are compact stories at caravan camps, representative outposts and existing settlements. Dunewell, Larksbridge, Three Roads and Reedhollow are represented through their people and facilities in the current playable areas; no additional full towns or continent traversal are claimed.

## Starts and progression

### The Unopened Cargo

Start with **Nima · Dunewell caravan** in **Frostmere**. Requires Stonewake.

1. Nima · Dunewell caravan: Accept the sealed cargo
2. North-pass cargo waymark: Carry the case onward without breaking the seal / Inspect the contents before carrying them onward
3. Broker Sal · Dunewell trading post: Swear the Runner oath · 140 gold and Superior enchanted boots

Final rewards (all are favorite-protected owned equipment and support the existing upgrade system):

- oath: 140 gold; Superior leather feet with fleet, named “Runner's Dust”; 6 leather. Runner oath sworn. The water claims reached Sal, and the families’ names stay off the market.

## Persistent consequences and scope

- The cargo seal decision and wage agreement are remembered. Cargo, samples and medicine are quest progress rather than general inventory items; they cannot be sold or discarded into a softlock.
- The mill wheel animates after repair and pauses with menus or travel. The water channel fills after repair. Venn relocates to the guesthouse with his nameplate. Medicine crates appear at the refugee camp after delivery.
- Refugees, the caravan cart, mill wheel, channels and document sites are visible props/NPCs. Venn’s travel, hired carriage, flour distribution and political administration are narrative/state changes, not walking escorts, simulated economies or working NPC AI.
- Runner, Broker and Concord Hand are derived from completed initiation/promotion quests and shown in the faction dossier. The earlier rank is required before the next quest.
- An outlaw Compact charter closes all unfinished Concord capstone actions. A Concord war charter closes Compact capstone actions. Once either finale is completed, the opposing hostile clause is unavailable. Peaceful finales work in either order, and contradictory completed saves are rejected.

## Implementation and saves

Content is in data/road_quests.json. scripts/road_quests.gd supplies physical sites, area-aware interactions, dialogue, costs, rewards and visual state; scripts/road_quest_rules.gd validates saved progress. Progress is optional hunt.roads, keyed by quest ID with an ordered answers array. Every saved answer must be an allowed option at that step, respect its branch, match required defeat evidence and satisfy story/faction prerequisites. The load path validates everything before applying it. Old saves without this field remain compatible.

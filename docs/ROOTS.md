# Pacts Beneath the Roots

The Stag Shrine is a playable Amberweald chapter after **Stonewake**. Take the western woodland trail from Frostmere. This branch is independent of the council and atlas; giving Pinehall's atlas to the Lantern does not close the grove.

## The woodland memory

Talk to **Maelin**, a Coven healer, or **Oren**, the woodland steward, in the two southern lodges. Accept the investigation and hear both proposals before making the eventual pact.

Three blightbound keepers defend the Spring, Stone and Seed roots. Defeat each root's keeper and cleanse the root with **two herbs and two water**. Roots may be restored in any order. The camp chest and northern gatherer's pack each contain four herbs, four water, four Heartwood and two Amber Resin. They provide enough cleansing supplies together, even with an empty starting inventory.

Cleansed roots glow green. All three must be restored before the **Hollow Warden** becomes active and vulnerable. Defeat it beneath the hollow tree, loot its named bow, then choose a permanent pact at the **Heart of the Stag**. Copy the woodland memory behind the shrine and report to either keeper.

The memory warns that the first crossing drew power from a living world. This evidence completes `roots`; it does not bypass the other convergence prerequisites or open the space expedition. This is a local woodland agreement. The separate [Briar Coven chain](COVEN.md) offers initiation and two promotions after this pact.

## Permanent choice and collection

| Reward | Properties | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amberbough Bow | 25 base damage; +2 Agility | Warden drops a favorited Superior bow of Fleet; either report unlocks its pattern |
| Groveheart Vest | 15 light armor; +2 Endurance; healing draughts restore 25% more health while worn | Wild pact: favorited Superior vest of Vigor and exclusive pattern |
| Pactkeeper Axe | 25 base damage; +2 Strength; melee kills restore 18 stamina, capped at maximum | Tended pact: favorited Superior axe of Fleet and exclusive pattern |

The wild pact restores flowering undergrowth and moves gathering beyond sacred ground. The tended pact adds ordered herb beds and promises controlled harvesting. These are visible authored changes, not farming or settlement simulation. The choice cannot be reversed, and only its exclusive equipment pattern is granted. Both reports also grant 140 gold, eight Heartwood, five Amber Resin and five leather.

Each new pattern costs four Heartwood, two Amber Resin and three leather at a forge. All three designs support tempering, enchantments, gems, favorites and salvage. Tempering uses leather and Heartwood; Exquisite and higher also require Amber Resin. Enemy loot and caches are finite. Existing shops elsewhere support buying and selling; the two woodland keepers are quest NPCs.

## Travel, saves and preview

The chapter has a forge, rune table, alchemy bench, local map, objective HUD and existing exploration/battle music with interaction sounds. Two enterable lodges lead toward copper-leaf trees, exposed roots and a hollow-tree shrine. Leaving restores the previous environment and resets surviving enemies; defeat safely returns the player home with restored vitals.

Acceptance, heard proposals, cleansing, pact choice, memory, rewards, caches, defeated enemies and claimed loot are stored in the optional `roots` character-save record. Invalid prerequisites, duplicate progress and contradictory records are rejected before applying a save. Older saves start with an untouched grove.

**Preview Stag Shrine.command** starts with Stonewake completed, a Superior steel sword of Fleet, a bow, arrows and healing supplies. Both character and atlas persistence remain disabled throughout the preview. E interacts, F heals, V switches camera, M opens the map.

See [the current content reference](CONTENT_REFERENCE.md) for catalog totals and quest availability. This chapter has not had timed human pacing tests or a new M1 performance benchmark.

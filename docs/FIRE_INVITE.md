# The Fire We Invite

The third Ashen Choir chapter is playable after either Cantor outcome. Ask Sereth about the bound ember. Defeat the existing choir_2 and choir_3 sentinels, then secure the west and east governors. Earlier defeats count. Decide at the new ember cradle beside the northern archive.

The captive ember is a local remnant; this quest does not open the campaign's great furnace or replace its seals. It reuses the sanctuary and existing sentinels, adding two governor interactions and a visible outcome-dependent core/caption. No new world, boss or simulated conquest is added.

Permanent choices all grant Ember Voice, 250 gold, 10 Resonant Ore and 8 Ash Glass:
- Awaken for conquest: Masterwork Fleet Resonance Blade, Ember Voice's Command. Closes the unfinished Iron Pact Clan Voice commission and mandate.
- Contain under guard: Masterwork Insight Cantor's Staff, Ember Voice's Vigil. The mountain finale remains available.
- Silence: Masterwork Vigor Amulet of Resolve, Ember Voice's Quiet. The mountain finale remains available.

All equipment is favorite-protected and uses existing upgrade systems. Conquest blocks both mountain commission acceptance and final mandate actions, including pending commissions with completed monster proofs. Earlier ranks, gear, shops and main-story progress remain intact. If Clan Voice was already completed, conquest is unavailable; both peaceful choices remain. Save validation rejects simultaneous completed Clan Voice and conquest. This ordering rule preserves earlier completed oaths instead of revoking rewards.

Optional choir fields: fire_started boolean, fire_seals unique fire_west/fire_east IDs, fire_choice empty/awaken/contain/silence. Validation requires Cantor completion before starting, guardian defeats for each secured control, both controls before choosing, and compatible final oaths. Legacy saves default to unstarted. Choices and rewards cannot repeat.

Validation: fire_invite 49, ash_memory 41, choir 70, clan_voice 21, mountain 74, atlas 42 = 297 passing checks. Native Mac finale decision and contained-core captures inspected. Fixtures disable normal persistence.
